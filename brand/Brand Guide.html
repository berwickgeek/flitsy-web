flitsy brand guide

The mark is {f|} — a lowercase f, framed in braces, with a cursor that blinks.

Built around three things: MCP / function-call vocabulary, a calm warm-cream ground borrowed from flitsy.app, and the cursor — the single "alive" element that signals you talk to Flitsy, not click around in it.

Anatomy

Four parts on one baseline. Scale them as a single unit.

1
Left brace — custom path
Not a font character. Sharp middle pinch, rounded terminals (miter join, miterlimit 10).
2
Lowercase f — two stroked paths
Stem + curl as one stroke; crossbar as another. Round caps. Crossbar stops short of the curl on the right.
3
Cursor bar — amber, rounded rect
~70% of f height. Blink: 1.1s, 50/50 duty cycle. Solid bar in static variants.
4
Right brace — mirrored left brace
Same path, flipped horizontally. Always paired — never use one brace alone.

Do & don't

Do
Use the mark whole — both braces, the f, the cursor.
Don't
f
No lone "f" in a rounded square — reads as Facebook.
Do
Invert to cream-on-ink for dark surfaces. Cursor stays amber.
Don't
No gradients on the cursor. Solid amber only.
